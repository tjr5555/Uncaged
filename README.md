# Uncaged

Uncaged is a physics-based platformer and adventure game set in a floating world made in Godot. You play as a bird, hopping across platforms on the ground and taking to the air to explore!

The goal is to pop balloons for coins, explore via. a vast network of procedurally generated worlds, and complete quests to level up.

--- 

## Movement

On the ground, your character can jump across islands and talk to NPCs. Switch to flight and your movement completely changes. Enjoy a comprehensive physics-based flight system with boosts, variable turning rate, and other features to make flying feel real and fun!

## Planned Features

- Seamless loading between portals
- Quest and progression system
- Advanced flight & movement system
- Procedurally generated worlds
- Original soundtrack made in FL Studio
- Fully handrawn art in Pixelorama
